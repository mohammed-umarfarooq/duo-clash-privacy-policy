1 Overview & General Principles

Duo Clash is a mobile game designed for casual 2-player local duels on a single screen and casual solo arcade play. It is developed and published by MUF Studios.

We believe in transparent, user-respectful privacy practices. All core minigames can be enjoyed offline locally on a single Android device. To support ongoing game development and provide the game for free without paywalls, the application integrates standard, industry-leading developer tools provided by Google LLC:

  • Google AdMob: Provides rewarded video ads, interstitial ads, and non-intrusive banner ads.
  • Google Firebase Analytics: Collects pseudonymous gameplay telemetry and engagement metrics to maintain game stability.
ℹ️
Key Takeaway: We do not operate external member databases or ask for personal credentials. Your in-game match progression and settings remain on your phone.

2 Information We Do Not Collect Directly

Unlike many mobile applications, Duo Clash does not maintain a user account system or require cloud authentication. Specifically, MUF Studios does not directly collect, request, store, or process:

  • Your real legal name, physical mailing address, or telephone number.
  • Your personal email address (unless you voluntarily contact our support team).
  • User account credentials, passwords, or social login tokens (no Google Sign-In, Apple ID, or Facebook Login required).
  • Financial, billing, or payment information (there are no in-app purchases or subscription credit card fields).
  • Biometric identifiers, contacts, SMS messages, or calendar entries.
  • Exact, precise GPS location coordinates.

3 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

When you play Duo Clash, certain game data is saved directly in your device's sandboxed private internal storage (specifically user://save_data.json). This information never leaves your phone and is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Player Nicknames: Local custom names entered for 2-player matches (defaulting to "Player 1" and "Player 2").
  • Match & Progression Scores: Win counts, high scores, completed levels, and earned stars across minigames.
  • Audio & Haptic Preferences: Sound effects volume slider, music volume slider, and vibration haptics toggle.
  • Daily In-Game Booster Timestamps: Daily hearts refill status and booster cooldown counters.
🔒
Cloud Backup Disabled: Duo Clash configures android:allowBackup="false". Your local save file is isolated to the application sandbox on your physical device.

4 Information Collected Automatically via Third-Party SDKs

While MUF Studios does not directly maintain servers or collect personal identifiers, the third-party software development kits (SDKs) integrated into the game collect and process certain technical data to deliver ads and telemetry:

A. Google AdMob (Advertising Delivery & Attribution)

Duo Clash integrates the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) with production ad units for rewarded videos, interstitial ads, and banners. AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Google Advertising ID (AD_ID / GAID): A pseudonymous, resettable identifier provided by Android OS for ad delivery, attribution, and frequency capping.
  • Device & Hardware Information: Device manufacturer, phone model, Android operating system version, mobile network carrier, and screen dimensions.
  • Coarse Geographic Location: General country or city-level location inferred from your IP address (precise GPS location is never accessed).
  • Ad Telemetry & Engagement: Ad impressions, clicks, video completions, and reward verification signals.
  • Performance Diagnostics: Ad loading latency, SDK errors, and network connection status.

AdMob uses this data to serve relevant advertisements, prevent ad fraud and click abuse, limit repeated ad exposures, and verify that in-game rewards (such as revives or boosters) are properly granted.

B. Google Firebase Analytics (Gameplay Telemetry & Performance)

The game integrates Google Firebase Analytics (Firebase BOM 33.7.0, Project ID: duo-clash-2e980) via a native Godot plugin to monitor overall app health, track milestone progression, and optimize minigame performance. Firebase automatically collects:

  • Firebase App Instance ID: A pseudonymous, randomly generated token unique to your installation.
  • In-Game Event Telemetry: Specific aggregated gameplay actions dispatched by the game:
    • app_launch: App opening event with game version code.
    • game_start: Minigame initiated, game mode (Solo, Duel, vs. Bot), and selected difficulty.
    • game_complete: Match finish, outcome (Win, Lose, Draw), and final score.
    • level_complete: Level number finished and stars earned.
    • booster_used: In-game booster triggered (e.g., Hint, Shuffle, Revive).
    • ad_reward_earned: Verified completion of a rewarded video ad.
  • Engagement & Technical Metrics: Number of app launches, active session duration, retention intervals, device model, and Android OS version.

Firebase Analytics does not link these gameplay events to your real name or personal contact information. No Custom User IDs or Properties: Duo Clash does not assign or transmit custom Firebase User IDs (setUserId is never invoked), nor does it configure or transmit custom user demographic properties.

5 Android Permissions Requested & Justification

Duo Clash requests a minimal set of Android permissions strictly necessary for ad loading, telemetry dispatch, and game tactile feel:

Permission Classification Technical Purpose & Justification
android.permission.INTERNET Standard Network Allows communicating with Google AdMob servers to fetch ads and sending gameplay telemetry events to Firebase Analytics.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Standard Network Allows checking whether an active Wi-Fi or cellular data connection is available before attempting ad requests.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Advertising ID Required on Android 13+ (API level 33+) for Google AdMob to access the Google Advertising ID for ad serving and attribution.
android.permission.VIBRATE Hardware Feature Provides subtle tactile haptic feedback during game collisions, button presses, and goal celebrations. Can be toggled off in settings.
✨
Zero Sensitive Permissions: Duo Clash does NOT request camera, microphone, contacts, storage access, or GPS permissions.

6 How Collected Information Is Used

The information collected through third-party services is used exclusively for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Game Operation & Maintenance: Ensuring minigames run smoothly across diverse Android hardware specifications.
  • Free Ad-Supported Access: Delivering ads that subsidize free gameplay without charging users for download or level advancement.
  • In-Game Booster Rewards: Verifying that a rewarded video ad has completed so the game can credit hearts, revives, or booster moves.
  • Gameplay Balance & Optimization: Analyzing gameplay milestone telemetry to balance minigame difficulty, verify ad completions, and ensure smooth performance across supported devices.
  • Fraud Prevention & Policy Compliance: Complying with Google Play Store and Samsung Galaxy Store policies regarding ad click fraud and invalid traffic prevention.

No Sale of Personal Data: MUF Studios does not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information or device identifiers to third parties or data brokers.

8 Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because of the privacy-by-design architecture of Duo Clash, you have full control over data retention and deletion:

1. Deleting Local Game Data

All local save data (high scores, progression, player names, settings) can be permanently erased by you at any time:

  • Open Android Settings → Apps → Duo Clash.
  • Tap Storage & Cache → select Clear Storage (or Clear Data).
  • Alternatively, simply uninstalling Duo Clash from your device immediately deletes all local app files.

2. Resetting Advertising Identifiers

You can reset or completely delete your Android Advertising ID at any time via Android system settings (see Section 12).

3. Firebase Analytics Data Retention & Deletion

Analytics event data logged in Google Firebase Analytics is retained according to standard Google Analytics data retention cycles (typically 2 to 14 months) and is then automatically purged by Google.

Pseudonymous Data Notice: Because Duo Clash does not require an account, collect personal identity details, or link analytics events to user email addresses, MUF Studios has no technical mechanism to identify, look up, or delete individual pseudonymous Firebase analytics records upon email request. Users who wish to reset or stop analytics tracking may clear the application's local data in Android Settings, reset their Advertising ID, or uninstall the application.

Depending on your country or region of residence, you may have specific statutory privacy rights under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA), or Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD):

  • Right to Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising: You have the right to opt out of interest-based ads (see Section 12).

11 Information Security

We take the security of your information seriously and implement multiple protective safeguards:

  • No Central Database Risks: Because we do not store player accounts or passwords in a central web database, there is no risk of cloud database breaches exposing user account credentials from our app.
  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All communications initiated by Google SDKs (AdMob and Firebase Analytics) are transmitted over industry-standard encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • Android Sandbox Isolation: Duo Clash operates within the Android operating system’s secure application sandbox, preventing other installed applications from reading your local match records or settings.

12 How to Opt Out of Personalized Advertising

You have full control over personalized advertising on your Android device. When you opt out or delete your advertising identifier, you will still see ads, but they will be contextual rather than tailored to your interests:

1
Open Android Settings

Launch the Settings app on your Android smartphone or tablet.

2
Navigate to Privacy or Google

Tap Privacy → Ads (or go to Google → Manage your Google Account → Data & Privacy → Ad Settings).

3
Reset or Delete Advertising ID

On Android 12, 13, and 14+, select Delete advertising ID to prevent any app from accessing your advertising identifier, or select Reset advertising ID to clear your previous advertising history.
